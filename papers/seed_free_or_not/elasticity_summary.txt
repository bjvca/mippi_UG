Demand for maize seed is estimated over the distribution of negotiated (final) transaction prices from the bargaining game (N=621), not a direct WTP elicitation.
The arc elasticity of demand computed over the randomized offer-price support (UGX 9,000 to UGX 12,000) is -7.00, but this number is a construction artifact: because P1_pric only ever takes the values 9,000/10,000/11,000/12,000, the paper's own exclusion of cases where the negotiated price equals the opening offer mechanically forces the survival share at 12,000 to zero, so this arc elasticity should not be quoted on its own.
The recommended headline number is the point elasticity of demand at the median negotiated price (UGX 7,000), computed from the local slope of the empirical survival function between adjacent UGX 1,000 price points (UGX 6,000 and UGX 8,000): -2.79 (cluster bootstrap 95% CI over 999 village-resampling reps: -3.28 to -2.37).
For comparison, the analogous point elasticity evaluated near the mean negotiated price (UGX 6612, nearest UGX 1,000 grid point 7000) is -2.79.
Recommended phrasing: 'the estimated elasticity of demand for maize seed with respect to the negotiated price, evaluated at the median transacted price of UGX 7,000, is [E_point_median] (95% CI [lo, hi], cluster bootstrap over villages).'
